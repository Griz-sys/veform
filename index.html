How it works. The report's header (PROJECT NAME, CONTRACTOR, ADDRESS, CITY / STATE, JOB NUMBER) fills the top boxes; each line item (units · description · mark #) drops into the grid, 23 rows a page, adding pages as needed. The %, D/E and Units Shipped columns are left blank to fill by hand.
